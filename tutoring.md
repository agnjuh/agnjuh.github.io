  <h2>Tutoring</h2>

  <p>
    I offer tutoring and academic support for secondary school, BSc, and MSc students in biology, chemistry, mathematics, biomedical science, and related subjects.
  </p>

  <p>
    Support can include help with understanding course material, preparing for examinations, planning assignments, scientific writing, data interpretation, and the development of dissertation or research projects. Sessions can be tailored to individual learning goals and levels of study.
  </p>

  <p>
    Sessions are available in English and Hungarian.
  </p>

  <hr>

  <h2>Areas</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Biology, chemistry, and mathematics (secondary school and university level)</li>
    <li>Biomedical science, molecular biology, cell biology, biochemistry, clinical biochemistry, genetics, and immunology</li>
    <li>Scientific writing, literature reviews, dissertations, and academic presentations</li>
    <li>Experimental design, data interpretation, and research project planning</li>
    <li>Introductory bioinformatics, R, Python, and reproducible analysis workflows</li>
  </ul>

  <hr>

  <h2>Format</h2>

  <p>
    Tutoring is available online and can be arranged as one-off sessions or ongoing support.
  </p>
