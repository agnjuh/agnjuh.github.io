  <h2>Tutoring</h2>

  <p>
    I offer tutoring and academic support for secondary school, BSc, and MSc students
    in biology, chemistry, mathematics, biomedical science, and related subjects.
  </p>

  <p>
    Support can include help with understanding course material, preparing for
    examinations, planning assignments, scientific writing, data interpretation,
    and dissertation or research project development.
  </p>

  <p>
    Sessions are available online in English and Hungarian.
  </p>

  <hr>

  <h2>Areas</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Biology, chemistry, and mathematics</li>
    <li>Biomedical science, molecular biology, cell biology, biochemistry, genetics, and immunology</li>
    <li>Scientific writing, literature reviews, dissertations, and presentations</li>
    <li>Experimental design, data interpretation, and research project planning</li>
    <li>Introductory bioinformatics, R, Python, and reproducible analysis workflows</li>
  </ul>
