  <h2>Tutoring</h2>

  <p>
    I offer online tutoring and academic support for secondary school students and BSc and MSc students in biomedical, clinical and related life sciences, tailored to individual learning needs and academic goals.
  </p>

  <p>
    Support includes understanding course material, examination preparation, scientific writing, data interpretation, and dissertation or research project development.
  </p>

  <p>
    Sessions are available in English and Hungarian. Selected subjects, including mathematics, programming and biomedical sciences, may also be available in Swedish.
  </p>

  <hr>

  <ul>

    <li>Molecular and cell biology, genetics, biochemistry and immunology</li>
    <li>Clinical biomedical sciences, including haematology, transfusion science, clinical biochemistry, microbiology and pathology</li>
    <li>Human anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology and pharmacology</li>
    <li>Precision and stratified medicine, pharmacogenomics and omics technologies</li>
    <li>Molecular biotechnology, bioinformatics and introductory machine learning</li>
    <li>Experimental design, research methods, data analysis, R and Python</li>
    <li>Scientific writing, literature reviews, dissertations and presentations</li>
    <li>Exam preparation, coursework support and research project planning</li>
    <li>Professional portfolio development for registration and licensure</li>
  </ul>
