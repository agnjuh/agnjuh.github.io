  <h2>Tutoring</h2>

  <p>
    I offer online tutoring and academic support for secondary school students
    and BSc and MSc students in biomedical, clinical and related life sciences,
    tailored to individual learning needs and academic goals.
  </p>

  <p>
    Support includes understanding course material, examination preparation,
    scientific writing, data interpretation, and dissertation or research
    project development.
  </p>

  <p>
    Sessions are available in English and Hungarian. Selected subjects,
    including mathematics, programming and biomedical sciences, may also
    be available in Swedish.
  </p>

  <hr>
